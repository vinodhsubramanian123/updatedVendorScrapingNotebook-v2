Core Architectural & Buildability Rules

1. Dual Processor (2P) Requirement for 32 Memory Slots

Each Intel Xeon 6 socket features 8 memory channels with 2 DIMM slots per channel (16 DIMM slots per CPU, 32 DIMM slots total across a 2P motherboard).

Populating 32 DIMM slots strictly mandates a dual-processor (2P) system because Socket 1 controls channels 1–8 (slots 1–16) and Socket 2 controls channels 9–16 (slots 17–32).

Populating 16 DIMMs per socket fills all 8 channels in a balanced 2DPC layout, running at 6000 MT/s.

To minimize base compute cost, two low-TDP Intel Xeon 6505P 12-core 150W processors (

P74503-B21

) with standard heatsinks (

P49145-B21

) are selected.
